Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Nesetto Digital ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use the PushDone app ("the App"). By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following information when you use the App:

  • Name and username – provided when you create an account
  • Email address – used for login and account management
  • User ID – a unique identifier for your account
  • List data – the lists and items you create in the App
  • Advertising data – used to show relevant advertisements

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and operate the App and its features
  • To enable list sharing with other users
  • To send local push notifications for reminders you set yourself
  • To display advertisements
  • To improve the App and fix issues

3. Local Notifications

Reminders you set in the To-Do List feature are stored locally on your device only and are not sent to any server. Notification permission can be revoked at any time in your iPhone's Settings.

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services, each with their own privacy policies:

  • Google Firebase – authentication and database storage (Privacy Policy)
  • Google AdMob – advertising (Privacy Policy)
  • Apple Sign In – authentication (Privacy Policy)
  • Google Sign In – authentication (Privacy Policy)

5. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely using Google Firebase, with servers located in the European Union. We take reasonable technical measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse.

6. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. You can delete your account at any time from the Settings screen in the App, which will permanently remove your personal data from our systems.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data (via the delete account feature in the App)
  • Withdraw consent to data processing at any time

9. Advertising

The App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may use your device identifier and other data to show personalized ads. In regions where consent is required (EEA/UK), the app will ask for your permission before loading ads. You can manage or withdraw consent at any time under Settings in the app.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by updating the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

11. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your data, please contact us at:

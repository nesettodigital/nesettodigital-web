Meet Pushfeud

Every day you get one chance to press the button. Miss a day and your streak is gone. Simple, addictive, and surprisingly hard to keep up.

Challenge your friends, build shared streaks, and compete on the global leaderboard in the Timer Game.

  • Daily streak tracking
  • Friend streaks & social features
  • Timer Game with global leaderboard
  • Chat with friends
  • Push notifications so you never miss a day

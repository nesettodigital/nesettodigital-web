Who it's for

PushPace is made for people who train in intervals and want the timer to stay out of the way.

Treadmill runners

Each interval has its own target speed and incline. PushPace reads them out, so you know what to dial in before the interval starts.

Outdoor intervals

Phone in your pocket, screen off. The spoken cues keep coming in the background, over your music.

HIIT, Tabata and EMOM

Start a classic format in one tap, or build your own with as many intervals and rounds as you like.

What it does

Everything below is included for free. Nothing is locked behind a paywall.

Unlimited custom workouts

Add as many work and rest intervals as you want, plus rounds, a warm-up and a cool-down. You see the total time before you start.

Voice cues, even with the screen locked

Each change is spoken out loud: what comes next, and the target speed and incline. Cues keep playing in the background and on the lock screen.

Works with your music

PushPace lowers Spotify, Apple Music or your podcast while it speaks, then hands the sound straight back. It never stops your music.

Speed and incline per interval

Set targets for every interval, shown in large type. Choose km/h, mph, min/km or min/mi and switch any time.

Distance, history and shoe mileage

Distance is worked out from your speeds, or typed in from the treadmill afterwards. Finished workouts are saved, and each pair of running shoes keeps its own mileage.

Readable from across the room

The whole screen changes colour with the phase, with a big countdown, a progress bar and what comes next. Plus a quick timer for 10, 20, 30 or 45 minutes.

Questions

Is PushPace really free?

Yes. Every feature is free, with no subscription and no locked features. The app shows a small banner and one ad after a finished workout, never during one. An optional one-time purchase removes all ads.

Do the voice cues keep playing when my screen is locked?

Yes. PushPace keeps speaking each interval change while the app is in the background and while the phone is locked, so you can run outdoors with the phone in a pocket or on an armband.

Can I listen to Spotify, music or podcasts at the same time?

Yes. PushPace briefly lowers your music while it speaks and then hands it straight back. It does not stop your music.

Can I set treadmill speed and incline for each interval?

Yes. Each interval can have a target speed and incline, shown in large type and read aloud. Speed can be shown in km/h, mph, min/km or min/mi.

Can I name each exercise, for example in an EMOM workout?

Yes. Every interval has a name that is shown in large type and read aloud when it starts. For an EMOM with kettlebells or dumbbells, add one 1-minute interval per exercise and name it with the reps, such as “12 kettlebell swings” or “10 goblet squats”, then set the number of rounds. Finish early and the rest of the minute is your break.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no login and no sign-up. Your workouts, history and shoes are stored on your phone. See the privacy policy for details.

Is PushPace available for Android or Apple Watch?

Not yet. PushPace is an iPhone app.
